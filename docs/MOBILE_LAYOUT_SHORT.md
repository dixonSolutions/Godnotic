# Mobile controls
The move stick stays in one place.
The stick is larger for easier movement.
Move with the left thumb and aim with the right.
Hold FIRE and drag to shoot while looking.
HOP and the hop-lock chip help sustain jumps.
ALT, zoom and hook appear when applicable.
Tap the weapon strip to switch weapons.
Menu, score and chat stay in the top bar.
Adjust sensitivity, size, opacity, handedness and fire mode in touch settings.
